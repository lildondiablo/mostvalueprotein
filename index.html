    ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    What food has the most protein per dollar?⌄
    Eggs consistently rank #1 at roughly 24g of protein per dollar, followed by chicken breast (~23g/$) and canned tuna (~20g/$). These three alone beat almost every restaurant and packaged food option.
    What fast food has the most protein per dollar?⌄
    The McDonald's McDouble leads at ~8.8g of protein per dollar, making it one of the best protein values at any restaurant. Little Caesars Classic Pepperoni pizza surprises many at ~10.3g/$, though the calorie count is high. Chick-fil-A's 12ct Grilled Nuggets (~7.8g/$) and Wendy's Grilled Chicken Sandwich (~7.5g/$) are strong runner-ups.
    How do I calculate protein per dollar?⌄
    Simple: divide total grams of protein by the price you paid. Example — a $4.35 chicken breast with 100g protein = 100 ÷ 4.35 = 23g per dollar. Use our calculator above to test any food instantly.
    Is Chipotle good value for protein?⌄
    It depends on what you order. A standard Chicken Bowl runs about 5.2g of protein per dollar — decent for a restaurant but far below grocery prices. Upgrade to a Double Chicken Bowl and you jump to ~6.5g/$, making it one of Chipotle's best value options.
    What's the cheapest way to hit 150g of protein a day?⌄
    A combination of eggs, chicken breast, and Greek yogurt gets you there for under $8–10 a day. Use our Stack Builder above to mix and match until you hit your goal without going over budget.
    Is protein powder good value compared to real food?⌄
    Whey protein powder typically lands around 9–10g of protein per dollar — solid, but not exceptional. Eggs and chicken beat it on protein per dollar. Where protein powder wins is convenience, portability, and zero cooking required. It's a good supplement to whole food, not a replacement.
